Numbered pieces fall from the sky. Tap two numbers and an operator to make the target shown at the top, before the pile reaches the red line.

Sounds easy. A few levels in, pieces fall faster, multiplication and division join in, and some pieces start to freeze over and hide their number.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap 2 numbers and 1 operator, in any order
• Hit the target and the pieces pop
• Chain equations for combos up to x4
• Don't let the pile reach the red line

SPECIAL PIECES
• Bomb: blows up everything around it
• Clock: stops pieces falling for 20 seconds
• Bulb: shows a solution
• Recycle: lightning that takes several pieces and changes the target
• Heat: a hot wave melts the ice on frozen pieces

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• Daily challenge: the same game for everyone, with a ranking
• Day streak with coffee cups and rewards
• 3 new missions every day
• Share your result with friends

MODES
• Classic: until the pile hits the line
• Zen: no red line, practise at your own pace
• Sprint 60s: as many equations as you can in one minute

AND ALSO
• Piece themes: Classic, Neon, Retro Pixel and Espresso, bought with coffee beans earned by playing
• 16 achievements
• Music that speeds up with the game
• High contrast mode and a less motion option
• Works offline (except the ranking)
• No ads and no real-money purchases

Great mental maths practice for kids and grown-ups.
